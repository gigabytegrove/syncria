# Syncria v0.6.0 — A whole new Syncria

Syncria now looks and feels like its own application.

This isn't another color change. The main screens were rebuilt around a new workspace designed for what Syncria actually does: keeping files moving between the places you choose.

## What will look different?

**A new Syncria identity.** Dark navigation, the familiar Syncria logo, bright blue and cyan accents, more confident text, and clearer layouts that work together across the app.

**A useful Home screen.** See your current syncs, connected storage, tracked files, and transfers at a glance. The new connection illustration shows what Syncria does; actual counts come from your saved configuration and real sync results. New users see the setup guide. Experienced users can get straight to their files.

**Syncs that make sense.** Every sync has its own flow view showing where files go, when Syncria checks, whether it's on or paused, and what happened last. Sync actions are together. Live transfer progress appears only while a sync is running. Conflict decisions and 10-minute deletion recovery remain available when needed.

**Storage, not a pile of forms.** Your NAS devices appear as a directory of connections. Adding a new NAS opens a focused setup area instead of filling the page with instructions and empty boxes.

**Google Drive and Dropbox each have a home.** Cloud accounts live in their own recognizable sections, with simple account actions. Multiple accounts remain supported.

**A clearer activity screen.** Real run results and file counts are grouped into a readable scoreboard and per-sync history instead of scattered statistics.

**Updates that behave like updates.** You still install from inside Syncria, with real bytes downloaded, file verification, and restart confirmation. A successful update briefly confirms completion and clears the status; failures retain their details.

**Better on small screens.** The same workspace adapts to phones and tablets, with accessible navigation, clear touch controls and readable text.

## What's staying exactly the same?

This release replaces the **interface**, not the synchronization engine. It keeps your existing accounts, NAS devices, folders, file-sync relationships, both-way and one-way options, the five conflict policies, per-sync deletion choices, the 10-minute Undo window, updater, rollback, and saved settings.

Syncria still streams your files between your cloud and NAS devices. It does not save copies of your files on the Syncria server.

## Update Syncria

Open **App Updates**, click **Check again**, then choose **Download & install update**. No manual compilation or extra updater container.

If you're upgrading from an older interface, refresh the browser once after the new Syncria version starts.

The friendly [Getting Started](GETTING-STARTED.md) guide and the [Technical Reference](TECHNICAL.md) remain available.
